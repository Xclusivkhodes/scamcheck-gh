Why we think so

{{i:hand-coins icon-sm}}

It asks you to send money back

“Pls I sent it by mistake, kindly send it back to 024 555 0123”

A known trick in Ghana. CSA and tech blogs warn about it. 1 2

{{i:message-square-text icon-sm}}

It came from a phone number

Real MTN cash-in alerts come from “MobileMoney”, not +233 55 000 4473. 3

{{i:clock icon-sm}}

It plays on kindness and hurry

“God bless you” and “Pls” push you to act before you check.
