# Name check — ScamCheck GH

**Recommendation: keep “ScamCheck GH” for the hackathon.** It is plain, says exactly what it does, and works for low-literacy users. Always write it with the **GH** (the gold pill in the logo). That is our local hook, and it sets us apart from the global apps below.

## What I found (quick web search, 25 Oct 2026)

| Conflict | Where | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Trend Micro ScamCheck** | Google Play (`com.trendmicro.fraudbuster`, 8K+ reviews), App Store, trendlife.com | **Medium.** A big security brand uses the same core word. Fine for a hackathon demo, but a risk if we ship to the Play Store under this name (trademark and search confusion). |
| **Scamcheck** by Abelssoft | iOS App Store (German developer) | Low. Generic, same idea. |
| **TrustGH**, **ScamVerifyGH** | trustgh.com, scamverifygh.com | Low on the name, but they are **direct local competitors** (number lookup + reports). In the pitch, our difference is: we check the *message itself*, with cited web evidence and visible AI steps. |
| “CheckAm” (rename idea) | checkamvin.com: Nigerian car VIN checker | Taken, but in another category. |

No exact match for “ScamCheck GH” or “ScamCheck Ghana” turned up.

## Clarity
- “Scam” + “Check”: both are everyday words in Ghanaian English. They pass the read-aloud test.
- Pronounce it as one word, **“scam-check G-H”**. In Twi copy, keep the brand in English.
- Write it as **ScamCheck** (camel case). Use “ScamCheck GH” in titles and app-store listings, and plain “ScamCheck” in running copy after the first mention.

## Taglines (pick one; the first is used in the board)
1. **Check before you send.** (primary: short, an action, works on MoMo and on links)
2. Strange message? Check it first. (hero headline)
3. Don’t be quick to send. Check first.
4. Twi option, *needs native-speaker review*: “Hwɛ ansa na woasoma.” (“Look before you send”)

## If we rename after the hackathon
Shortlist to check properly first (trademark + Play Store): **ScamCheck GH** stays the strongest. Alternatives: *Hwɛ* (“look” in Twi; short, local, needs a check), *MoMo Guard* (avoid: it suggests MTN’s product), *CheckFirst GH*.
